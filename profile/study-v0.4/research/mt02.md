# MT-02 primary-source audit and Korea budget evidence

Checked 2026-10-25 KST. Research only. No vendor contact, purchase, CAD change, or Git change performed. Files here are ignored research intermediates.

## Conclusions the public report can safely state

1. MT-02 is a materially different design from MT-01: twin geared NEMA17 motors, twin SFU1605 screws, 4080 metal frame, aluminum load-bearing mounts, Arduino Mega, touchscreen. The official thesis is Stella Demmel (TU Wien,2024), DOI10.34726/hss.2024.113842.
2. The thesis specifies a3.5kN goal and computes3.388kN available axial force. The comparison experiment is five PETG specimens per machine with MT02 mean peak800.346N. It does not demonstrate a calibrated2kN or3.5kN operating range. Preserve2kN as our design/validation target, not proven inherited rating.
3. AppendixA.1 has an exact parts list and fastener list, but no prices or total. The official MT01 README underEUR500/1.5kN claim must not be applied to MT02.
4. The selected currently listed near-original Korea procurement basket already sums to about577,160KRW before metal fabrication, several mandatory parts, and delivery/tax unknowns. A dual-direct-NEMA23/3.2touch alternative gives about514,773KRW before the same missing lines. Neither is a complete sub500,000KRW quote. A cheaper Ali-heavy design may be possible but has not been established by verifiable selected-option checkout prices.
5. Native MT02 FreeCAD/PCB/firmware repository and an applicable design license were not located. The thesis says FreeCAD was used; this does not establish downloadable native sources. A CAD recreation should be called MT02-inspired reconstruction from published dimensions, never authentic MT02 CAD.

## What the thesis actually validates

Printedpp27–28/PDF38–39: thrust calculation3.388kN; the practical travel speed is capped450mm/min for noise, not because of a measured load-speed envelope.

Printedpp35,39–40/PDF46,50–51: manual test0.5–6mm/min, force/time/travel output at5Hz; no separate position sensor, displacement comes from commanded step count. One motor can be jogged independently to realign the crosshead. Movement is hold-to-run on the touchscreen. Force limit is used in manual test mode; travel and other modes ignore load-cell readings. The described overload indications include stepper knocking and possible bottom-beam sliding at bracket joints. These are not a demonstrated calibrated mechanical overload limiter.

Printedp42/PDF53: calibration used a0.5kg reference mass, tare before each specimen,30–40Npreload and1mm/min test speed. The reused MT01 grips haveM8threads while MT02 was designed forM12; improvised washers/M10spacers andM12-toM8adapter were used. Grip/adaptor procurement is missing from the Appendix table.

Printedpp48–56/PDF59–67:fivePETGtests per system; strength agrees approximately1percent, but corrected crosshead-derived modulus0.717GPa versus Zwick video1.483GPa is under by48percent. Corrected ultimate strain7.581percent versus3.869percent is roughly96percent higher. Original raw crosshead-based modulus0.342GPa is even lower. This cannot be sold as accurate modulus/strain measurement without specimen-level optical tracking or a validated compliance correction. A two-sample t-testp=.690 withn5 is not a general equivalence certificate. The thesis has small internal discrepancies for Zwick mean strength40.642/40.648/40.842MPa; report approximately40.4versus40.8MPa instead of overclaiming the final decimal.

AppendixA.2 printedpp64–65/PDF75–76:INA219 current sensing was tried to detect missed steps and discarded because it could not detect them. A4988 was rejected for heat/low speed;DM320T was rejected for abnormal speed behavior. Do not add INA219 to the final validated sensor list or imply a closed-loop force/position system.

Engineering implication (our inference): a low-mass calibration extrapolated to800Ncomparison does not verify2kN/3.5kN. Before claiming2kN, bench verification must cover mounting/joint stiffness, gripper slip, twin-drive synchronization, force zero/span/repeatability/hysteresis through the intended range, and independent measured crosshead travel. A camera can retain a low-cost specimen-strain path, but a purchased camera or calibration target must be budgeted unless already owned.

## A defensible route toward the500kconstraint

First prioritize an engineering budget ceiling rather than falsely claiming a purchase-ready BOM. At500kall-in, components must cost materially below500kto leave delivery, machining andfixture reserve. The verified dual-motor basket does not achieve that.

Potential future costdown: oneNEMA23+oneDM542Tdriving both screws with a2:1timing belt; lower-cost matchedscrew/supportkits; simplemetalplates using ownedtools; keepS-cell/HX711/USB/touch; use an existingcamera for strain. This changes thedrive topology and independent motorjog is replaced by mechanicalphasealignment. The belt/couplers/mounts needdesign andnewprocurement quotes, and motorholding torque cannot prove2kN. Do not present this unquoted concept as retaining every original function or as completeunder500k.

For original-equivalentassembly, credible budget is above the577kpartialbasket by the missinglines; no precisecomplete total is justified yet. For a500kstrict cap, currently justified status is procurement feasibility unconfirmed, not impossible and not established.
